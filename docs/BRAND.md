# Brand

## Name

**Burnline** is the line that shows how quickly local agent usage is accumulating. It is short, technical, and useful without pretending to be an intelligence layer.

Write it as one word with normal title case: **Burnline**. Do not append “AI dashboard” to the product name.

## Mark

A single live waveform crosses a glass lens and ends at a small terminal point. The shape represents separate local agent streams reduced to one readable signal.

The full app icon uses an indigo glass tile, a white waveform, and one cyan endpoint. The same compact full-color mark appears in the menu bar so Burnline remains identifiable among monochrome utilities. Pressing it gives one restrained scale response; Reduce Motion disables that response.

Keep clear space equal to the endpoint diameter. Never add a robot, brain, magic wand, chat bubble, or decorative sparkle.

## Palette

- Indigo `#5E5CE6` — primary signal and glass tint
- Cyan `#38B8F2` — optical endpoint highlight
- Cloud `#F5F7FF` — light foreground
- Midnight `#10131B` — dark icon field
- Green `#30D158` — healthy local read
- Orange `#FF9F0A` — source issue

The app itself uses semantic system foregrounds and materials. Brand colors tint small elements; they do not replace native light/dark adaptation.

## Type

Use San Francisco. SF Pro Rounded is reserved for the spend total and brand name. SF Pro Text carries labels and metadata. Avoid display fonts, all-caps wordmarks, and monospaced body copy.

## Voice

Short, factual, and calm. Say “partial estimate,” not “you saved.” Say “no local usage,” not “connect your account.” Avoid exclamation marks, mascots, gamification, and fake urgency.

## Motion

The waveform moves slowly while idle and becomes quicker during refresh. Motion communicates state; it does not perform for attention. No bounce, orbiting particles, loading spectacle, or staged card entrances.
